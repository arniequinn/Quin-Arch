## Draft pitch email

**To:** info@bimheroes.com

**Subject:** Guest post idea — what LOD tiers actually mean for outsourced BIM scoping

Hi BIM Heroes team,

I'm Arslan Qaiser, a PCATP-registered architect and BIM/computational specialist who's spent the last
several years delivering Archicad BIM production (LOD 100–350, LOD 400 by request) and CD sets for firms in the US, UK,
Canada, and Australia (portfolio: https://quinarch.design/).

I'd like to pitch a guest article for BIM Heroes: **"What 'LOD 350' Actually Means When You're
Hiring Outsourced BIM Help — And Why Most Scope Disputes Start Here."**

Most scope disagreements between firms and their outsourced BIM/CAD partners don't come from bad
work — they come from an undefined LOD tier. I'd walk through the LOD 100–400 spectrum from the
delivery side, with real examples of where each tier's line actually falls (hood chase
coordination, MEP shaft routing, coastal wind-load documentation), so a firm scoping their first
outsourced engagement knows exactly what to ask for.

It'd be written for your audience, not as a pitch for my own services — happy to keep any
self-reference to a single byline link. I can have a draft (~900–1200 words) to you within a
week of a yes.

Let me know if this is a fit, or if there's a different angle for your readers that would work
better.

Best,
