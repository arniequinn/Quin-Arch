# Outreach Email Templates

Written 2026-09-26 for §9 of `businessmodelandoutreach.md` (the active 90-day plan). Copy, fill
in the `[brackets]`, and send from your own email. Track every send in
`documentation/private/outreach-tracker.csv`, and track the client asks in section 5 in
`documentation/private/client-asks-tracker.csv`. That folder is git-ignored because the repo
is public; back it up somewhere else, such as Google Drive. Each CSV opens in Excel or Google
Sheets. Delete the `EXAMPLE` row before you start.

## Rules for every email

- **Keep it short.** Under 120 words. A principal at a small firm reads it on a phone between
  site visits.
- **Include one real sentence about their firm**, taken from their website, a recent project or
  a LinkedIn post. The sentence that proves you looked matters more than any other line. If
  you can't write one, skip the firm.
- **Include one link**, matching the work to their project type (see the segment table). Never
  send the homepage and three other links.
- **Make one ask:** the free test sheet (firms and builders), a 20-minute capacity call, or a paid render pilot (developers) — never more than one.
- **Tag every site link** with `?utm_source=outreach&utm_medium=email&utm_campaign=overflow` (change `utm_source` per channel, e.g. `linkedin`) so GA4 shows which emails lead to calls and test sheets.
- **Voice:** senior, specific and calm. No "affordable", no percentages saved, no "I hope this
  email finds you well", and no platform names (Upwork, Fiverr).
- **Legal basics:** use your real name and practice, give an honest subject line, and include
  the postal address and opt-out line in the signature. Honour an opt-out the same day and
  mark it in the tracker. Only email addresses the firm publishes. Start with US, UK and
  Australian firms; Canada's rules (CASL) are stricter.
- **Follow up once**, 5–7 days later, then stop.
- **Send from** `arslan@quinarch.design` once it is set up; until then use
  `arslan.qaiser1991@gmail.com`. Never use `faizanqaiser9@gmail.com` for the practice.

## 5. Existing clients: referral, review and permission to quote (§9 step A)

Send one message per client, personally. Not a mass email.

**Subject:** A small favour, and thank you

> Hi [First name],
>
> Thank you again for the work on [their project]. Clients like you coming back is the best
> part of what I do.
>
> I'm growing the practice beyond word of mouth, and three things would help a lot. Any one is
> great:
>
> 1. **A short review on Clutch.** They'll email you a quick form or a 10-minute call. Okay if
>    I send you the request?
> 2. **Permission to quote you** on my website by name and firm, e.g. "[a line they've said to
>    you before]". I'll send you the exact wording first.
> 3. **An introduction.** Is there one firm or builder you know who's stretched for drafting or
>    render capacity right now? A one-line intro email is plenty.
>
> No pressure at all, and thank you either way.
>
> Arslan

**If you first met this client on Upwork or Fiverr:** read that platform's current terms
before sending. Asking for a review or quote is lower risk than asking to take work off the
platform. Upwork's no-going-around rule runs for 24 months from first contact (confirm the
current rule), so clients from 2022 or earlier may be outside it. Until you have checked,
leave item 3 out for platform clients, or ask them to refer the contact to you *on* the
platform.

**When they say yes to Clutch:** in the Clutch dashboard, use "Get reviews" (or "Request a
review") to send the reference request to their email.

## 6. LinkedIn connection note (optional, under 300 characters)

Send to principals at firms on your outreach list, ideally a few days *before* the email.

> Hi [First name]. I'm an architect working with small firms on permit sets and BIM. I liked
> [specific project] on your site. Would be glad to connect.

No link and no pitch in the connection note. The email does that.

## 7. Reply handling

| They say | You do |
| --- | --- |
| "Send details / what would it cost?" | Reply the same day. Ask for the drawings or brief for one real task and quote the pilot. Offer a 15-minute call. |
| "Not now, maybe later" | Thank them. Set a follow-up for 60 days later in the tracker's `next_action_date`. |
| "We already have someone" | Thank them. Ask if you can check in when they hit a crunch. Mark as `closed`. |
| "No" / "unsubscribe" | Reply with nothing, or a single "Understood, removed." Mark `opted_out = Y`. Never email again. |
| No reply after the follow-up | Mark as `no_reply`. Stop. |
