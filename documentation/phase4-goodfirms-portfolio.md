## 1. Cran Residence — Traditional Two-Story Home

**Image:** `public/portfolio/cran-perspective.jpg`

**Description:**
Classic two-story red brick residence with a pitched roof, bay-fronted entry, and fenced front
garden — modeled from the client's hand sketches into a coordinated 3D BIM massing study for
early design sign-off ahead of full construction documentation. The model let the client validate
massing, roof pitch, and facade proportions before committing to detailed drawings, catching
proportion issues early instead of in redlines.

**Tags:** Traditional Residential, 3D BIM Massing, Brick Facade

---

## 2. Parametric Facade & Computational Design

**Images:** `public/portfolio/bimcad-workflow/09-diagrid-pattern-script.jpg`,
`06-facade-paneling-script.jpg`, `11-facade-closeup-render.webp`

**Description:**
Computational facade studies built in Rhino/Grasshopper — diagrid structural patterning and
panelized cladding logic generated parametrically rather than modeled panel-by-panel, so
geometry changes propagate automatically instead of requiring a full rebuild. This is the kind
of scripting-driven production most outsourced BIM/CAD shops don't offer — the closeup render
shows the panelization resolved down to individual panel joints, not just a massing concept.

**Tags:** Parametric Design, Rhino/Grasshopper, Facade Engineering, Computational Design

---

## 3. Barn Residence — Exterior Visualization

**Image:** `public/portfolio/barn-residence-vray.jpg`

**Description:**
Photoreal exterior visualization of a barn-style residence, rendered in V-Ray from a coordinated
3D model — used for early design approval and marketing material before construction. Demonstrates
the full pipeline from model to client-presentation-ready render, not just working drawings.

**Tags:** Visualization, V-Ray Rendering, Residential Design
