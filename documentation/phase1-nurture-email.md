# Phase 1 Item 7 — Nurture Email (Draft)

A single manual reply, not an automated drip sequence — Web3Forms' free tier doesn't do
autoresponders, and a real automation platform (Mailchimp etc.) is a bigger lift than this list
currently justifies. The fix: whenever the "New LOD Guide Submission" notification lands in
Gmail, reply to that visitor directly within a day or two using the template below. Low effort,
still closes the loop instead of the list sitting unused.

---

## Reply template

**Subject:** Following up on the LOD guide

Hi [Name],

Thanks for grabbing the LOD reference guide — hope it's useful for scoping your next project.

If you're currently weighing an outsourced BIM/CAD partner or just want a second opinion on what
LOD tier a project actually needs, happy to take a quick look — no charge, no obligation. Just
reply here or use the estimator on the site: https://quinarch.design/

Best,
Arslan Qaiser
PCATP-registered architect (A-07767), Quintessential Architecture

---

## Notes

- Keep it short and non-salesy — the guide itself already did the "value delivered" work; this
  email's only job is to open a door, not close a sale.
- If a submission clearly isn't a real inquiry (spam, obviously irrelevant), skip the reply —
  don't burn time replying to every row.
- This closes out Phase 1 item 7. No further automation needed unless volume grows enough to
  justify a real ESP later.
